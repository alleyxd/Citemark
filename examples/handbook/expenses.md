# Expense policy

Submit expenses within 30 days of the purchase, with a receipt, through the finance portal.
Meals while travelling are covered up to $75 per day. Alcohol is not reimbursed.
Economy class is the default for flights under 6 hours; business class needs director approval.

# Home office

New employees can claim up to $800 once for home-office equipment such as a chair, desk or monitor.
Equipment over $300 per item stays company property.
