# Time off

Full-time employees get 20 days of paid vacation per calendar year, plus statutory holidays.
Vacation accrues monthly and up to 5 unused days carry over into the next year; the rest expire on December 31.

# Sick days

There is no fixed limit on sick days. For absences longer than three consecutive working days, a doctor's note
is required. Sick days do not reduce vacation balance.

# Parental leave

The company tops up government parental benefits to 90% of base salary for the first 16 weeks.
